# Combined Experiment 1 — transition length and conditional multimodality

Combined 8 fixed noisy states across 4 paired endpoints; the original and expanded cohorts remain unchanged.

| Δt | states favoring K=2 over K=1 | fraction | mean ΔBIC ± SD | median ΔBIC [IQR] |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0200 | 1/8 | 0.125 | 27.74 ± 19.89 | 30.06 [20.68, 43.57] |
| 0.1000 | 1/8 | 0.125 | 23.40 ± 49.68 | 36.59 [28.83, 41.93] |
| 0.1496 | 3/8 | 0.375 | 4.84 ± 58.57 | 18.50 [-13.93, 46.57] |
| 0.2496 | 4/8 | 0.500 | -10.91 ± 67.72 | 7.67 [-32.96, 26.28] |

Evidence for a K=2 alternative becomes more prevalent with transition length (1/8, 1/8, 3/8, 4/8), but state heterogeneity is large: three states remain unimodal at every endpoint and one is already multimodal at the shortest endpoint.
